About JTS CAD CAM Wax Casting

Bridging Heritage Gold Craftsmanship with Sub-Micron Precision

JTS CAD CAM Wax Casting is a premier engineering hub for jewelry CAD Matrix design, 3D resin wax printing, and lost-wax vacuum casting.

Whether you are a wholesale retail jeweler seeking precision 22K gold casting trees or an individual client creating a one-of-a-kind bridal choker, our master designers convert complex 2D concepts into flawless 3D castable structures.

⚙Precision MaterialsHigh-density castable resin and engineered sprue systems.
✦Core ServicesCAD matrix, SLA/DLP wax printing and vacuum lost-wax casting.
📍 Serving Vijayawada, Guntur, Vizag, Hyderabad, & Pan-India Exporters
